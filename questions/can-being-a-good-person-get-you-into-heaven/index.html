Can being a good person get you into heaven?

No. Heaven isn't earned by being good enough, because none of us meets God's standard. It's a free gift: if you accept Jesus as your Lord and Savior, you're a 10. If you don't, you're a 1. There's no in between.

Watch on YouTube

The 1 to 10 question

Ask someone: on a scale of 1 to 10, if you died right now, how sure are you that you'd go to heaven? Most people say a 6 or a 7. They don't want to sound proud, but they think they're a good person who helps people and does good things.

Why a 6 or a 7 misses the point

Any answer but a 1 or a 10 shows we don't understand who God is and what He did for us through Jesus. It isn't about how good a person we've been. None of us has lived up to God's standard, and He knew that.

God already had a plan

God knows the beginning from the end. When He made us, He knew we would fall, and He already had a plan to show us His great love. When we accept Jesus, we will be with Him in paradise.

Questions people ask

Do good people go to heaven?

Not because they're good. No one meets God's standard on their own. Heaven is a free gift through Jesus.

How can I know I'm going to heaven?

Accept Jesus as your Lord and Savior. Then you're not a 6 or a 7; you're a 10, because of what He did, not what you did.

What if I've done more good than bad?

It isn't a scale of good against bad. One sin is enough to fall short, which is why we need Jesus.
