Privacy Policy: Channel Analytics

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Channel Analytics is a private tool used by The Adam Redeemed Podcast to read the performance of its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public, and it is used only by the account owner.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in and allows access, Channel Analytics reads, read-only:

  • YouTube: the channel's uploaded videos (titles, descriptions, durations, publish dates); the channel's own analytics for them (views, watch time, average percentage viewed, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained, audience retention, traffic sources, search terms, viewer age, gender, country and device in aggregate, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate); and the public comments left on the channel's videos.
  • Instagram: the account's own posts, reels and stories with their insights (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, skip rate, follows, profile visits), account-level insights and follower demographics in aggregate, and the comments left on the account's posts.

Comments include the commenter's public display name or username and the comment text. On YouTube the permissions requested are the read-only youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly, plus a key that can only read public YouTube data. On Instagram they are instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_manage_insights and instagram_business_manage_comments. Channel Analytics never uploads, posts, replies, likes, hides, deletes or changes anything on YouTube, Instagram or either account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner how their own content is performing (a dashboard, a weekly review and alerts) and to sort comments so the owner can read and answer them personally, for example questions to answer in a future video and prayer requests. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to contact anyone, and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's own computer. Sign-in credentials are kept in the computer's secure keychain; the analytics data and comments are kept in local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Google access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose Channel Analytics, then remove access). Instagram access can be removed in the Instagram app under Settings, Website permissions, Apps and websites (remove Channel Analytics). Deleting the local data files and the keychain entries removes everything Channel Analytics stored.

If you commented on The Adam Redeemed Podcast's videos or posts and want your comment removed from these local records, email adamredeemedpod@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days. Deleting your comment on YouTube or Instagram also stops it being read again.
