Privacy Policy: Channel Analytics

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Channel Analytics is a private tool used by The Adam Redeemed Podcast to read the performance of its own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and it is used only by the channel's owner.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and allows access, Channel Analytics reads, read-only:

  • the channel's list of uploaded videos and their titles, descriptions, durations and publish dates (YouTube Data API);
  • the channel's own analytics for those videos: views, watch time, average percentage viewed, likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained, audience retention, and thumbnail impressions and click-through rate (YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs).

It requests only the read-only permissions youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly. It cannot upload, edit, delete, comment, or change anything on YouTube or in the Google account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner how their own videos are performing: a dashboard, a weekly review and alerts about videos that are doing unusually well. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer. The sign-in credential (a Google refresh token) is kept in the computer's secure keychain; the analytics data is kept in local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose Channel Analytics, then remove access). Deleting the local data files and the keychain entry removes everything Channel Analytics stored.
