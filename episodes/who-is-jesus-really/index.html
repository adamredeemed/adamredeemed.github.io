Who is Jesus, really?

Jesus is God who came as a man. He lived a perfect life, never sinned, and took the penalty for every sin on the cross so we could be made clean before God again. If you believe in God, Jesus is not someone extra: He is God, and He is the way back to Him (John 14:6).

Why the podcast is called Adam Redeemed

The first Adam broke what God made. Jesus is called the last Adam because He fixed it and redeemed it. It's also personal: my name is Adam, and God has redeemed me.

Why would God become a man?

Sin separates us from a holy God, and the Bible says the wages of sin is death (Romans 6:23). Someone had to pay that penalty. God loved us too much to let it be us, so He limited Himself, came as a human, lived 33 years without sin, and took the punishment Himself.

It also means we can never say to God, "You don't know how this feels." He lived it: a quiet working life as a carpenter, then hatred, false accusations and the cross, from the very people He made.

If I believe in God, why do I need Jesus?

Jesus answered it Himself: "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one can come to the Father except through me" (John 14:6). The only thing standing between us and God is the sin on us, and Jesus is the atonement for it.

So the way in is not being a good enough person. It's confessing that Jesus is Lord, believing God raised Him from the dead, and turning away from sin (Romans 10:9). Good works follow because we want to please Him; they aren't the ticket.

What God sees after you come to Him

"Though your sins are like scarlet, I will make them as white as snow" (Isaiah 1:18). Love keeps no record of wrongs (1 Corinthians 13:5). The thief on the cross had no time to fix his life, but he recognised who Jesus was, and Jesus told him he'd be with Him in paradise that day (Luke 23:43).

Why money, fame and everything else still leave you empty

Every one of us wants to be loved. Money, sex, attention and fame promise to fill that and never do. We were made for a relationship with our Creator, and without it something is always missing. With Him, like Paul said, whether you have little or much, you have what you need (Philippians 4:12).

Questions people ask

Is Jesus God?

Yes. Jesus is God who came in human form, lived without sin, died for our sins and rose again. Loving God and loving Jesus are the same thing.

Can I go to heaven if I believe in God but not Jesus?

Jesus said no one comes to the Father except through Him (John 14:6). His death pays for our sin; accepting Him as Lord is how that payment becomes ours.

Can being a good person get you into heaven?

No. One sin is enough to separate us from a holy God, and no amount of good deeds pays for it. Heaven is a free gift through Jesus: on a scale of 1 to 10 you are either a 10 in Christ or a 1 without Him.

Do I have to fix my life before I come to God?

No. He is the hospital, not the reward for getting well. Come today as you are; He changes you over time.
