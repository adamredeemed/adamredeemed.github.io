Channel Analytics

Channel Analytics is a private tool the podcast uses to read how its own YouTube videos and Instagram posts are doing: views, watch time and which videos people share. It only reads the channel's own numbers, can't change anything on YouTube or Instagram, and keeps everything on the podcast's own computer. It isn't offered to anyone else.
