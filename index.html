About Adam

I'm Adam Ascensao. For about six years I ran a furniture business in the Boston area that I thought was my future. Then I became convinced God was telling me to shut it down and start a podcast about Jesus, the last thing I'd ever have picked for myself.

I grew up Catholic and went through all the classes, but nobody ever showed me what any of it had to do with my life. Years later God brought me to a Pentecostal church, and that opened my eyes big time. I've been following Jesus for about five or six years now, and it changed me for good.

This show is for people with honest questions: believers, skeptics, and anyone who has wondered why Jesus matters. No pretending to have it all figured out. Just hard questions, answered straight.

Why "Adam Redeemed"? What the first Adam broke, Jesus, the last Adam, came to fix. And my name is Adam.
